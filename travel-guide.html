Getting There

How to Reach Varanasi

By Air

Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS)

22 km from city center. Well connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, and other major cities. Direct international flights from select countries.

Airport to City: Taxis, app-based cabs (₹400-600), Airport bus available

By Train

Varanasi Junction & Banaras

Major railway stations with excellent connectivity. Varanasi Junction (Cantt) is the main station. Many daily trains from all major Indian cities.

Popular Trains: Shatabdi Express, Rajdhani Express, Duronto Express, Vande Bharat

By Road

Bus & Private Vehicle

Well connected by NH2 and NH7. Regular buses from nearby cities. Private taxis and buses available from all major destinations.

Distances: Delhi (820km), Lucknow (320km), Allahabad (125km), Gaya (230km)

Plan Your Visit

Best Time to Visit

Winter (October - March) - BEST SEASON

Temperature: 5°C - 15°C (Dec-Jan), 15°C - 32°C (Oct-Nov, Feb-Mar)

Why Visit: Pleasant weather, perfect for sightseeing, Dev Diwali, Mahashivratri, comfortable boat rides

Peak Season: November (Dev Diwali), February-March (Mahashivratri, Holi)

Summer (April - June) - HOT

Temperature: 25°C - 45°C

Note: Very hot and humid. Not recommended for outdoor activities. Early morning and evening visits only.

Monsoon (July - September)

Temperature: 25°C - 35°C

Note: Heavy rainfall, high water levels in Ganga. Some ghats may be submerged. Beautiful but challenging for tourists.

Getting Around

Local Transportation

Auto-Rickshaws & Taxis

Most convenient for city travel. Agree on fare beforehand. App-based cabs: Ola, Uber available.

Avg Fare: ₹50-200 within city

Boat Rides

Best way to see ghats. Sunrise boat rides highly recommended. Book at ghats or through hotels.

Cost: ₹200-500 per person per hour

Walking

Best for exploring narrow lanes and ghats. Most attractions within walking distance if staying near ghats.

Tip: Wear comfortable shoes

Plan Your Trip

Ideal 3-Day Varanasi Itinerary

Day 1 — Ghats & Ganga Aarti

Sunrise boat ride on the Ganga, walk Assi to Dashashwamedh, visit nearby lanes, and watch the evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Day 2 — Temples & Old City

Kashi Vishwanath darshan, Sankat Mochan or Durga Temple, Banarasi silk shopping, and street food like chaat, lassi, and paan.

Day 3 — Sarnath & Slow Banaras

Half-day trip to Sarnath’s Buddhist sites, then return for a quieter ghat sunset, classical music evening, or second aarti viewing from a boat.

Common Questions

Varanasi Travel FAQ

What is the best time to visit Varanasi?

October to March is ideal for pleasant weather, ghat walks, temple visits, and evening Ganga Aarti. Major festivals like Dev Diwali usually fall in this season.

How many days are enough for Varanasi?

Two full days cover the essentials: sunrise boat ride, Kashi Vishwanath, and Ganga Aarti, plus Sarnath. Three days is more comfortable for markets, food, and quieter ghat time.

Where can I watch the Ganga Aarti?

The main evening Ganga Aarti is at Dashashwamedh Ghat. Arrive early for steps seating, or book a boat view. Morning rituals are popular at Assi Ghat.

How do I reach Varanasi?

Fly into Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS), or arrive by train at Varanasi Junction / Banaras station. Local autos, taxis, and boats help you move between ghats and temples.

Essential Travel Tips

✓ Dress Modestly: Wear respectful clothing when visiting temples and ghats.

✓ Early Morning Visits: Best time to experience ghats is sunrise (5-8 AM).

✓ Bargain: Always negotiate prices for shopping, auto-rickshaws, and boat rides.

✓ Stay Hydrated: Drink bottled water only. Carry water bottle always.

✓ Beware of Touts: Avoid unofficial guides and touts at railway stations and tourist spots.

✓ Photography: Ask permission before photographing people or inside temples.
